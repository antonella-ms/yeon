Ye-on Bot — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ye-on Bot ("Ye-on", "the Bot", or "the Service"), created and maintained by Miu, collects, uses, and stores information when you use the Bot.

By using Ye-on, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Ye-on may collect and store information necessary for the Bot to function properly, including:

- Discord User ID and username.
- Discord server IDs and relevant server information.
- User inventories, collected cards, virtual currencies, and other game-related progress.
- Bot settings and preferences.
- Information related to commands and interactions with Ye-on when necessary to provide its features.
- Basic usage information that may help us identify errors, abuse, or technical problems.

Ye-on does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as passwords, payment information, private messages, or real-world addresses.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected by Ye-on may be used to:

- Provide and maintain the Bot's features.
- Save and manage cards, currencies, inventories, and other user progress.
- Provide support and investigate reports or problems.
- Detect and prevent abuse, exploits, cheating, or unauthorized use.
- Fix bugs and improve the Bot.
- Develop and test new features.
- Maintain the security and stability of Ye-on.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or intentionally share users' personal information with third parties for advertising or commercial purposes.

Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable laws, legal obligations, or valid requests from authorized authorities.

Information may also be accessible to authorized Ye-on staff when necessary to provide support, moderate the Bot, investigate abuse, or maintain its services.

4. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as it is reasonably necessary for Ye-on to provide its features and maintain user progress.

If certain information is no longer necessary for the operation of the Bot, it may be deleted or removed.

Users may contact the Ye-on support team through the official support server if they have questions or requests regarding their stored data.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information stored by Ye-on against unauthorized access, modification, or deletion.

However, no online service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security of information.

6. Third-Party Services

Ye-on operates through Discord and may rely on third-party services such as hosting, databases, or other technical services necessary for the Bot to operate.

The handling of information by those services may also be subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Children's Privacy

Ye-on is intended for users who meet Discord's minimum age requirement and any applicable age requirements in their country.

Ye-on does not knowingly request or intentionally collect sensitive personal information from minors.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to Ye-on, its features, or its data practices.

When significant changes are made, the updated version may be announced through the official Ye-on support server.

Continued use of Ye-on after changes to this Privacy Policy means that you acknowledge the updated policy.

9. Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your information, you can contact the Ye-on support team through the official Ye-on Discord server.
